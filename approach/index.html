Approach

Research broadly. Understand vertically. Propose one workflow.

Infralyte is an AI transformation practice, not a SaaS-only lead machine. The market is any organisation with document-heavy, repetitive, analytical, or knowledge-intensive work.

What you get

A customized recommendation on top of a reusable method. We do not arrive with a single product and then hunt for a place to put it.

Individual professionals can receive a productized workspace and onboarding. Mid-market organisations receive workflow integrations. Enterprises receive governance, private deployment, SSO, evaluations, and staged rollout.

We already run document-heavy operations and agentic workflows internally. That is how we stay fast: we use AI to build AI. When a client cannot send data to a public model, the same lab that serves our own work — NVIDIA DGX Spark plus Apple Silicon — is how we prove a private path.

What we will not do

  • Auto-send outreach, scrape personal inboxes, or buy leaked lists.
  • Present model guesses as facts.
  • Replace a CA, lawyer, clinician, or other qualified human on a regulated decision.
  • Name confidential consulting customers to win a meeting.
